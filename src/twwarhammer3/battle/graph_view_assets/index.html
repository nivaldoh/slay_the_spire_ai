        How to read this view
        

          Positions come from public observations. They are not interpolated. Enemy positions appear
          only in frames where the enemy was visible. The graphs are the ones saved in this battle’s
          controller card.
        

        

          Event time is no earlier than its source observation and the latest observation already
          collected at its trace row. Orders also use their dispatch time; acknowledgements also use
          their application time. These are recorded bounds, not measurements of Python execution
          time.
        

        

          “Accepted” means the engine accepted an order. A graph reports completion separately,
          using its recorded witness. Repeated completions can describe an existing engagement. A
          local cancellation sends no halt order. Replay speed changes only this offline view.
        

        

          This view does not score graphs, infer missing effects or establish tactical strength or
          learning.
