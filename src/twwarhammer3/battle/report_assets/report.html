Metric definitions & limitations

        
Active-strength margin
Friendly active men ÷ starting men, minus the same enemy fraction. Routing and shattered men count as lost. Units are not weighted by value. Means use valid trials with available metrics.

        
Measured information age
Battle time from the observed state to order issuance or queuing, averaged over non-empty batches per trial. This includes model, network and retry delay; issuance does not confirm execution.

        
Controlled added age
The minimum requested age of the buffered snapshot, separate from the actual age at decision and order issuance. Conditions are compared within recorded evidence groups; missing conditions are not interpolated.

        
Cost per attempted trial
Total recorded inference USD divided by started trial records, including failed trials. Setup-only jobs have no trial denominator. Provider costs may be self-reported; incomplete cost records are unavailable in the chart and displayed mean. Scripted controllers have zero inference cost.

        
Independent launches
Distinct job instances in a controller’s trial records. Rematches within a launch do not add independent evidence. The scenario status counts launches with started trials; all supplied jobs are listed separately.

        
Protected-unit survival
Active men left in the scenario’s declared protected unit types, divided by their starting men.

        
Objective-control shares
Victory/key-point control from recorded objective summaries. Current records integrate engine time; legacy records use sampled observations. The table identifies the method. Supplies are the recorded amount left; reinforcements count accepted purchases by unit type, not confirmed arrivals.
